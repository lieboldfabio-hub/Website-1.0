Company

Efficient connections in raw material trading

S&F Materials supports companies in sourcing and trading raw materials and secondary raw materials. Our aim is to bring suitable business partners together and to accompany procurement processes efficiently and professionally.

What we do

A trading and intermediary partner, not a warehouse

S&F Materials sources material against a buyer’s specification and brings suitable suppliers and industrial buyers together. We do not hold stock and we do not publish availability. Each enquiry is worked on individually, from the specification through to the point where both parties can talk to each other directly.

Our counterparts are companies: recyclers, processors, foundries, mills and traders. All business is B2B.

Scope

Where we work

The focus is deliberately narrow. It grows with the network, not ahead of it.

  • Materials

    Copper, aluminium and further metallic secondary raw materials. Additional materials are added as demand and supply develop.

  • Regions

    Suppliers and industrial buyers across Europe. Enquiries from outside Europe are reviewed case by case.

  • Counterparts

    Companies only: recyclers, processors, foundries, mills and traders. No sales to private individuals.

Approach

What you can expect

Our services
  • A clear answer, including when we cannot serve a requirement
  • Specifications agreed before material is offered
  • Confidential handling of enquiries and counterparties
  • Direct contact between the parties once a match is found
  • No obligation until commercial terms are agreed

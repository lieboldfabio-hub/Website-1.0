Scope

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit this website and when you send us an enquiry, on what legal basis, and which rights you have. Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Hosting provider, company and address. When you access the site, the provider’s servers automatically record information that your browser transmits. This includes:

  • the address of the page requested and the referring page
  • browser type and version, operating system
  • date and time of the request
  • the IP address in shortened or full form

This data is required to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and security of our website. The data is deleted after retention period of the hosting provider. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

Contact form and enquiries by email

If you send us an enquiry through the contact form or by email, we process the data you provide – company, name, email address, the material and quantity you are looking for and the content of your message – in order to answer your enquiry.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where the processing serves the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering enquiries addressed to us. The data is deleted once it is no longer required, unless statutory retention periods apply – commercial correspondence is subject to retention obligations under German commercial and tax law.

Cookies, analytics and external services

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not embed content from social networks or advertising networks. No consent banner is therefore required.

Fonts are hosted on our own server. No connection to Google Fonts or any comparable external service is established when you visit this website, and no data is transmitted to such providers.

Data transfer to third parties

We only pass on your data where this is necessary to process your enquiry – for instance to a potential supplier or buyer – and only with your agreement, or where we are legally obliged to do so. Names of counterparties are not disclosed without prior agreement.

Encryption

This site uses TLS encryption for security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the address bar of your browser switching to „https://“.

Your rights

You have the right, at any time and free of charge:

  • to obtain information about the data we store about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • to have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • to have your data erased (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • to have processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • to receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • to withdraw consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

A request to the address above is sufficient. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is competent supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy

We adapt this privacy policy when the website changes or when new legal requirements make it necessary. The current version always applies.
